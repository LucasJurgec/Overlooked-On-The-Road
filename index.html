× The Story

The data flips the common assumption.

When people think about road crash risk, they picture young drivers — P-platers, late nights, inexperience. But the BITRE hospitalisation data from 2011 to 2021 tells a different story.

Adults aged 40–64 consistently account for the highest hospitalisation counts of any age group. Motorcyclists in this cohort are particularly over-represented — and the gap widens significantly in regional and remote Australia.
